Our School

Rooted in Airdrie since 1864

A Catholic, comprehensive and co-educational secondary school serving Airdrie and communities across the surrounding area.

Our story

St Margaret’s first opened on Hallcraig Street in 1864. The present purpose-built school, between Drumbathie Road and Waverley Drive, was officially opened in 1974.

The school is the Catholic secondary school serving Airdrie and surrounding communities including Chapelhall, Plains, Caldercruix, Greengairs, Longriggend, Glenmavis and Salsburgh.
🏫

Campus

The main teaching block has five floors and includes a lift. Specialist wings support science, art, drama, music and technical subjects.

The PE wing includes a games hall, three gyms and a multi-purpose theatre/PE area, with nearby sports facilities used by arrangement with North Lanarkshire Council.
