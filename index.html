Welcome

A community of faith and learning

The school describes its mission as supporting young people to develop their talents, become well-rounded adults and move confidently into further or higher education, training or employment.

✦

Faith & values

A Roman Catholic school community shaped by Gospel values, partnership, respect and service.

📚

Learning & achievement

A broad six-year secondary experience with a focus on attainment, achievement and progression beyond school.

🤝

Partnership

Families, pupils, staff, partner schools and the wider community are encouraged to work together.
